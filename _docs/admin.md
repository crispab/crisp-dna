Admin and day-to-day operations
===============================

Crisp has no employees at all. So there is no office staff and no admin department either. The non-consulting work that keeps Crisp running is split between an external accounting firm, the [board](board.html), a few [Crisplets](crisplets.html), and the Crispers themselves.

Who does what?
--------------

-   **Invoicing, payments and bookkeeping** - an external accounting firm sends our invoices, handles incoming and outgoing payments, follows up on unpaid invoices, does the bookkeeping, and keeps the Crisplet Accounts. This is part of [Crisp Core](core.html), so every Crisper pays for it through the Core fee. The [board](board.html) oversees the accounting firm.
-   **Finances, the bank account and legal duties** - handled by the [board](board.html).
-   **Handling incoming email and phone** - done by our sales professional, who routes client requests according to the [bun protocol](bun-protocol.html). This is part of [Crisp Core](core.html).
-   **Agreements and contracts** - our sales professional reviews and signs agreements, also for Crispers who are not in the Sales Crisplet. This is part of [Crisp Core](core.html) too. The [board](board.html) signs some contracts.
-   **The unconference** - the planners and facilitators of each [unconference](unconference.html) are elected at the previous one, and they organize it.
-   **Courses and events** - the Producer of each course does the course admin, with voluntary technical support from other Crispers. Course producers form the Course Crisplet. See [Courses and event production](courses-and-event-production.html).
-   **The office** - run by the Office Crisplet. Its members manage the office (keys, cleaning, furniture, etc), pay for it, and have priority when using it. All Crispers have access to the office, paid for by a contribution from [Crisp Core](core.html).
-   **Onboarding new Crispers** - one of the Crispers takes this on voluntarily.
-   **Everything else** - someone who cares about it does it, or forms a [Crisplet](crisplets.html). If nobody wants to do it, maybe it doesn't need doing.

What about sales?
-----------------

Sales is a joint effort. Many assignments come through our own networks and reputation, and our sales professional works on opening up new opportunities and supports the whole sales process, from first contact to signed agreement. Crispers who want more help can opt in to the Sales Crisplet. See [Sales](sales.html).
