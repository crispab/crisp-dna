Crisp Core
==========

Crisp Core is the set of basic services that every Crisper gets, and pays for, by being part of Crisp. Everything beyond Core is opt-in.

What is included in Core
------------------------

- **Finance function** - an external accounting firm handles our invoicing, incoming and outgoing payments, and bookkeeping.
- **The board** - the [board](board.html) is paid for running the company: finances, legal duties and overseeing the accounting firm.
- **Insurance** - a shared liability insurance that covers us in our client work, and insurance for some of the equipment in our office.
- **Marketing and brand building** - our website and the [Crisp brand](brand-building.html), which bring in most of our leads.
- **Shared leads** - client requests are routed between all Crispers via the [bun protocol](bun-protocol.html).
- **Sales support for everyone** - our sales professional does some work that benefits all Crispers, not just the members of the [Sales Crisplet](sales.html): handling incoming email and phone, and reviewing and signing agreements. Core pays the Sales Crisplet a share of its costs for that.
- **Office access** - every Crisper can use our office in Stockholm. The office is run by the Office Crisplet, and Core pays it a contribution for everyone's access.
- **Community** - being part of something bigger than the sum of its parts.

The Core fee
------------

Core is paid for by the Core fee: a fixed fee per month plus a percentage of your revenue, with a yearly ceiling. See [How Crisp works](how-crisp-works.html) for how the fee is designed and why. Like all our fees it is only meant to cover costs, so it is adjusted up or down as needed.

See [Economic model](economic-model.html) for concrete examples of how the fee is invoiced.

Opt-in services
---------------

Some services are only wanted by some of us. Those are opt-in, and only the Crispers who use them pay for them, through a separate fee on top of the Core fee. For example:

- **Sales** - access to our sales professional and additional sales support, see [Sales](sales.html).
- **Engineering** - for Crispers who love software engineering.
- **Office** - priority over other Crispers when using the office. Members of the Office Crisplet also run the office. Booked courses always come first, though, whoever produces them.

Long-lived opt-in services like Sales, Engineering and Office are run as [Crisplets](crisplets.html). See [Admin and day-to-day operations](admin.html) for who handles what.

Why Core + opt-in?
------------------

We want a shared base of services, but we don't want everything to be mandatory. [Freedom](what-is-crisp.html) is one of our core values, and that includes the freedom to choose which services you need, and to only pay for what you use.

If an opt-in service turns out to be something everyone needs, we can move it into Core. And if something in Core turns out to be needed only by some, we can make it opt-in.
